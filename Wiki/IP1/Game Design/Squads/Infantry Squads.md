These descriptions are designed for Cobra, your WWII multiplayer FPS, with a grounded, historically inspired tone similar to Hell Let Loose. Each role has a short description suitable for the squad selection UI and a longer description for tooltips or class information panels.

## 1. [[Squad Leader]]
COMMAND / COORDINATION

![[Pasted image 20261109143727.png]]

**Short Description:** Leads the squad, coordinates battlefield movements, and establishes forward spawn positions.

**Full Description:** The Squad Leader is responsible for directing their squad through the battlefield. Equipped with leadership tools and a versatile weapon, they coordinate attacks, designate objectives, communicate with command, and establish forward spawn positions. Effective leadership is essential to maintaining squad cohesion and achieving victory.

## 2. [[Rifleman]]
STANDARD INFANTRY / VERSATILE

![[Pasted image 20261109143359.png]]

**Short Description:** A versatile frontline soldier equipped for reliable combat at medium and long ranges.

**Full Description:** The Rifleman forms the backbone of every infantry squad. Armed with a standard-issue service rifle, they excel at engaging enemy infantry across varied terrain. Their straightforward equipment and adaptable combat capabilities make them effective in both offensive and defensive operations.

## 3. [[Assault]]
CLOSE QUARTERS / OFFENSIVE

![[Pasted image 20261109160958.png]]

**Short Description:** Specializes in aggressive assaults, clearing enemy positions, and close-quarters combat.

**Full Description:** The Assault soldier excels at breaking through enemy defenses and engaging hostile forces at close range. Equipped with automatic weapons and grenades, they are particularly effective when clearing buildings, attacking fortified positions, and leading aggressive infantry advances.

## 4. [[Automatic Rifleman]]
SUPPRESSIVE FIRE / MOBILE FIREPOWER

![[Pasted image 20261109161344.png]]

**Short Description:** Provides mobile automatic fire to suppress enemy infantry and support squad advances.

**Full Description:** The Automatic Rifleman delivers sustained firepower while maintaining the mobility needed to advance alongside the squad. Armed with a squad automatic weapon, they suppress enemy positions, cover friendly movements, and provide a critical advantage during infantry engagements.

## 5. [[Medic]]
MEDICAL / SQUAD SURVIVABILITY

![[Pasted image 20261109162005.png]]

**Short Description:** Treats wounded soldiers, revives incapacitated teammates, and keeps the squad fighting.

**Full Description**: The Medic is responsible for preserving the squad's fighting strength by treating battlefield casualties. Equipped with medical supplies and a personal defense weapon, they can revive incapacitated teammates and restore wounded soldiers to combat readiness. Their presence allows squads to sustain prolonged engagements without unnecessary losses.

## 6. [[Support]]
LOGISTICS / RESUPPLY

![[Pasted image 20261109162745.png]]

**Short Description:** Supplies essential resources and ammunition to sustain squad operations and battlefield construction.

**Full Description:** The Support soldier provides the logistical foundation necessary for sustained combat operations. Carrying supplies and ammunition, they enable engineers to construct defensive positions, assist squad leaders with battlefield infrastructure, and help maintain the squad's operational effectiveness during extended engagements.

## 7. [[Machine Gunner]]
HEAVY FIREPOWER / AREA DENIAL

![[Pasted image 20261109162520.png]]

**Short Description:** Deploys sustained machine-gun fire to suppress enemy formations and control critical terrain.

**Full Description:** The Machine Gunner provides the squad with heavy suppressive firepower. Equipped with a belt-fed or other sustained-fire machine gun, they excel at defending strategic positions, denying enemy movement through exposed terrain, and supporting infantry assaults. Effective positioning and coordinated fire make them a formidable force on the battlefield.

## 8. [[Anti-Tank]]
ANTI-ARMOR / SPECIALIST

![[Pasted image 20261109162157.png]]

**Short Description**: Engages enemy armored vehicles using specialized anti-tank weapons and explosives.

**Full Description:** The Anti-Tank soldier specializes in neutralizing enemy armored threats. Equipped with anti-armor weaponry, they can disable or destroy hostile vehicles and protect friendly infantry from armored advances. Successful anti-tank operations require careful positioning, knowledge of vehicle vulnerabilities, and coordination with nearby squad members.

## 9. [[Engineer]]
CONSTRUCTION / DEMOLITIONS

![[Pasted image 20261109161715.png]]

**Short Description:** Constructs defensive fortifications, deploys battlefield obstacles, and destroys enemy infrastructure.

**Full Description:** The Engineer shapes the battlefield through construction, fortification, and demolition. Equipped with specialized tools and explosives, they can establish defensive positions, deploy obstacles, construct resource infrastructure, and destroy enemy fortifications. Their abilities are essential for strengthening defensive lines and supporting coordinated assaults.
