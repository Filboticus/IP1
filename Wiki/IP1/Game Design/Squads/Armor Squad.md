Three-person armor squads.

**Armor Squad Role Types**
1. [[Tank Commander]]
2. [[Crewman]] - Driver
3. [[Crewman]] - Gunner

![[Pasted image 20261109142741.png]]

Inspired by Hell Let Loose, these descriptions are designed for your WWII multiplayer FPS project, Operation Cobra (IP1). Armor squads consist of three specialized crew members working together to operate tanks and other armored vehicles effectively.

## 1. [[Tank Commander]]
Squad Leadership / Coordination

![[Pasted image 20261109141956.png]]

Leads the armored crew, identifies enemy threats, and coordinates movement and engagements. Responsible for battlefield awareness, target prioritization, and communicating with other squads to maximize the tank's effectiveness.

## 2. [[Crewman]] — Driver
Vehicle Operation / Positioning


![[Pasted image 20261109140918.png]]

Operates the armored vehicle, navigating difficult terrain and positioning the tank for combat. Responsible for maintaining mobility, protecting vulnerable armor, and responding quickly to the commander's directions.

## 3. [[Crewman]] — Gunner
Firepower / Target Engagement

![[Pasted image 20261109141113.png]]

Controls the tank's primary weapon, engaging enemy armor, infantry, and fortified positions. Responsible for accurate targeting, ammunition selection, and delivering decisive firepower under the commander's direction.

**Armor Squad Role Definitions**

| Role           | **Primary Function**           | Squad Limit | Vehicle Position                  |
| -------------- | ------------------------------ | :---------: | --------------------------------- |
| Tank Commander | Command and Target Identifican |      1      | Any Seat, typically commander     |
| Crewman        | Drive, Load and Fire Gun       |      2      | Driver, gunner, or commander seat |
